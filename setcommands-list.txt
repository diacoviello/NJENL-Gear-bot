help - How things work around here
settopic - Assign commands to a topic: /settopic cmd1 cmd2
removetopic - Remove a topic assignment: /removetopic cmd1 cmd2
need - Post a gear request
have - Post a gear offer
needs - List open gear requests
offers - List available gear offers
filled - Mark a need as filled: /filled id
cancel - Withdraw an offer: /cancel id
clearneeds - Mark all your open requests as filled
clearoffers - Withdraw all your open offers
run - Volunteer to transport gear: /run need_id offer_id
runs - List your active transport runs
delivered - Mark a transport run as delivered: /delivered id
rat - Mark someone as a rat: /rat @username
unrat - Clear someone's name: /unrat @username
rats - See who is on the rat list
rank - Check your rank in the Family
promote - Promote someone to Soldier (reply to their message)
family - See the full Family roster
pitch - new bot feature proposal: /pitch 
unpitch - remove a proposed idea: /unpitch 
smurf - Roast a blue agent: /smurf agentname
ideas - list all proposed bot features
